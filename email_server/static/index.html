The FiveNights CRM Workflow in 4 Steps:

Our CRM is designed specifically for B2C player retention, VIP account management, and automated re-engagement across FiveNights.fun. Follow this 4-step sequence:

Step 1 Infrastructure
Connect Google Workspace Mailboxes

Connect your 20 Google Workspace paid accounts via smtp.gmail.com:587 with 16-character Google App Passwords. Each account handles up to 100 emails/day to guarantee 100% primary inbox delivery.

Step 2 Lead Management
Manage Customer Pool & Claim Leads

John (Admin) imports customer CSVs or clicks "Distribute Leads" to evenly split unassigned accounts. Agents browse the "Unassigned Pool" and click + Claim to add customers to their own portfolio.

Step 3 Outreach
Fast 1-Click Outreach / Campaigns

Click ⚡ Send on any customer row for 1-click B2C messaging (VIP Concierge, Win-Back, Tier Upgrade, Check-In), or check multiple boxes to launch an automated campaign queue.

Step 4 Unified Inbox
Reply & Retain via Unified Inbox

When customers reply, they appear immediately in the Unified Inbox. Agents use 1-Click Reply Chips to reply instantly via their assigned Google Workspace SMTP account.

John (Managing Director & Administrator) Controls:

As the administrator, John has executive power to manage all customer data, import files, assign mailboxes, and balance team workloads.

1. How to Import Customers from CSV:

Click "Import CSV" in Overview or Customers tab. You can either upload a .csv file or paste raw text rows. Supported headers: email, name, company/username, phone, country. Delimiters supported: comma (,), semicolon (;), or tab.

2. How to Distribute Leads Evenly:

In the Overview tab, click "Distribute Leads". The system will find all unassigned customers and divide them equally between Max, Fred, and Chriss.

3. How to Auto-Assign Google Workspace Mailboxes:

Click "Auto-Assign Mailboxes". Your 20 Google Workspace addresses are partitioned across the 3 account agents in round-robin fashion so each agent has dedicated sender addresses.

4. Team Pulse Live Monitoring:

Click "Team Pulse" to see live agent metrics: number of assigned customers, connected mailboxes, pending unread conversation threads, and emails sent today.

Check the Inbox tab throughout the day. When a customer replies, click their conversation. Use the Quick Reply Chips (VIP Reload, Win-Back Bonus, Payout Priority, Concierge Check-In) to auto-fill your message, then click Send Reply!

20 Google Workspace Accounts Strategy (Variant B):

Why use 20 Google Workspace mailboxes? By spreading your email dispatches across 20 distinct paid Google Workspace accounts, no individual mailbox exceeds 50–100 emails/day. This ensures 99%+ primary inbox placement and protects your sending domain.

Step-by-Step: How to Generate a Google App Password (Required for SMTP):
  1. Log in to your Google Workspace account and go to myaccount.google.com/security ↗
  2. Make sure 2-Step Verification is turned ON.
  3. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords ↗
  4. Enter App name: FiveNights CRM and click Create.
  5. Google will show a 16-letter App Password (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop). Copy and paste this into the CRM!
⚠️ Do NOT use your normal Google login password! Google blocks normal passwords on direct SMTP connections for security. Only the 16-character App Password is accepted.

What is the default login password?

The default password for all 4 accounts (John, Max, Fred, Chriss) is 347581. You can also click your profile button on the login screen for 1-click instant login!

Why can't I edit another agent's notes?

To prevent accidental overwrites and maintain clear audit trails, agents can only edit notes they personally authored. John (Admin) has master permission to edit or delete any note.

What is Simulation (Dry-Run) mode?

In Settings, when Simulation mode is active, the entire campaign flow (template rendering, variables, anti-spam delay calculations) runs without dispatching live emails. When you are ready for real delivery, toggle it to Live.

How do I switch which agent profile I am sending from?

Click your agent badge in the top right header, or go to the Agents tab and click "Select Profile" on any agent card.
